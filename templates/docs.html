    Getting started

    Create your account

    1. 1 Press Launch app, then Create an account (or Start free under Plans on the home page).
    2. 2 Type your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
    3. 3 Open the email we send and press Confirm my account on the page it opens.
    4. 4 Sign in.

    Confirm within {{ verify_minutes }} minutes. After that the unconfirmed account is removed and you can simply sign up again.

    Opening the link alone does nothing: you must press the button. This stops company mail scanners, which open every link, from confirming accounts nobody asked for.

    Sign in and passwords

    • Remember me keeps this browser signed in for {{ remember_days }} days.
    • Forgot password? on the sign in page emails you a link to choose a new one. It works for {{ reset_minutes }} minutes. Setting the new password signs out every device; then sign in with it.
    • After {{ fail_limit }} wrong passwords in {{ limit_minutes }} minutes, signing in pauses and the page says how many minutes to wait. A browser you signed in with before has its own count, so strangers guessing your password cannot lock you out there.
    • New passwords that appear in known data breaches are refused.

    Years and your base

    Nomad Life counts in solar years, 1 January to 31 December. Each year is its own workspace with a base: the city and country you live in, usually where you pay tax.

    Create a year

    Your first sign in opens Set up your year. Later years start from the + Year tab on the dashboard.

    {{ shot('year-new', 'The Set up your year form with Year 2027, Base country Portugal and Base city Lisbon filled in', 'Example: 2027 with the base in Lisbon, Portugal.') }}

    You can have one workspace per year. The tabs next to + Year switch between them.

    Change your base or delete a year

    On the dashboard, Manage base and contract (on the Base card) lets you change the base city and country, upload your rental contract, or delete the year. Deleting a year removes its movements and receipts and asks you to type the year to confirm.

    Movements

    A movement is a stay away from your base: a trip, a month in another city, a flight day. Log each one with its country, city and dates.

    Add a movement

    {{ shot('movement-new', 'The New movement form: after typing "ita" in Country, the list offers Italy, Mauritania and United Kingdom; numbered markers point at Country, the To date and the Add movement button', '1 Pick the country, 2 set the dates, 3 press Add movement. A receipt can be attached right away.') }}
    • Press + Add movement on the dashboard.
    • Both dates count. From 1 March to 3 March is 3 days.
    • The dates must fall inside the year. A trip over New Year is two movements: 28 to 31 December in one year, 1 to 5 January in the next.
    • Notes are optional, up to {{ '{:,}'.format(notes_max) }} characters, and only you see them (the accountant package leaves them out unless you ask).

    The country picker

    Type part of a name, a two letter code or a common name: ita finds Italy, JP finds Japan, Holland finds Netherlands. Accents do not matter. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or tap a country.

    A place that is not in the list can be typed as it is. It is counted as its own country and listed under the map.

    Edit or delete a movement

    Press the city in the Movements table, or Edit. Delete removes the movement and its receipts after two confirmations.

    {{ shot('delete-dialog', 'The delete confirmation dialog on step 2 of 2: "Are you sure? This permanently deletes the movement to Madrid and its 2 receipt(s)." with Cancel and Yes, delete permanently', 'Every delete asks twice. The second step says what will be removed.') }}

    Limits: city and country up to {{ place_max }} characters, and up to {{ '{:,}'.format(movements_max) }} movements a year.

    How days are counted

    Every day of the year goes to one country at most. Four rules decide which one:

    1. Movements count in full, planned future ones too.
    2. Days without a movement count for your base, but only once they have passed (today included). Empty future days count nowhere yet.
    3. A day in two movements (an overlap), such as a travel day, counts for the one that started later. If both start the same day, the shorter one wins; with identical dates, the newest.
    4. A movement inside your base country counts for the base country.

    When movements overlap by more than a travel day (one ends the day the next begins), a red triangle blinks next to the Movements title (until you open its report). Press it to read which movements share which days and where those days count, copy the report, and fix the dates if you were not in both places.

    A worked example

    Base Lisbon, Portugal. Today is 10 March. Spain from 3 to 6 March, Italy from 6 to 8 March, and a trip to Thailand planned for 13 and 14 March.

    The {{ threshold }} day line

    Many countries treat you as a tax resident once you spend {{ threshold }} days or more there in a year. The Days in base country card marks that line: it says At least {{ threshold }} days, or how many days you are short. In Days per country a bar turns red when another country reaches {{ threshold }} days.

    Nomad Life counts days; it does not give tax advice. Rules differ by country (some count partial days or look at other ties), so check yours with an accountant.

    Receipts

    Keep the proof of where you were: rental contracts, hotel bills, flight tickets.

    Upload a receipt

    {{ shot('receipts', 'The Receipts card of a movement: a type menu set to Flight ticket, a chosen file named flight-lis-bcn.pdf and the Upload button, above two saved receipts', 'Choose the type and the file, then press Upload.') }}
    • Upload on a movement's page, or on the base page for your rental contract.
    • Types: {{ kinds() }}.
    • Files: PDF, PNG, JPG, HEIC or WebP. Files are checked by their content, so a web page renamed to .pdf is refused.
    • Big JPG, PNG and WebP photos (over {{ photo_megapixels }} megapixels or {{ photo_shrink_mb }} MB) are resized to at most about {{ photo_megapixels }} megapixels and saved as JPEG without their metadata, which removes the location a phone stores in a photo. They are never made narrower than {{ '{:,}'.format(photo_min_side) }} pixels, so long screenshots stay readable. HEIC files, smaller photos and photos over {{ photo_max_megapixels }} megapixels are stored as they are, metadata included.

    Download, rename or delete

    Download ZIP gives the receipt inside a ZIP file. Rename or change type opens a small form under its name. Delete asks twice.

    Storage

    The Receipt storage card shows the space left, shared by all your years. Your plan sets the total and the largest single receipt (see Plans). When storage is full, delete receipts you no longer need, or upgrade.

    Settings

    Open Settings from the menu under your name.

    Name, password and email

    • Profile: your first and last name, shown in the menu, in emails and on the accountant package.
    • Change password: needs your current password. You stay signed in here; other devices are signed out.
    • Change email: we send a link to the new address, valid {{ email_change_minutes }} minutes, and nothing changes until you open it. Your old address gets an alert with an Undo this change link, valid {{ revert_days }} days, in case it was not you.

    Two-factor sign in

    {{ shot('two-factor', 'The Two-factor sign in card with a QR code (blurred here), three setup steps, the Code from the app and Current password fields and the Turn on two-factor sign in button', 'Scan the code with your authenticator app, then type the 6 digit code it shows.') }}
    1. In an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Bitwarden) add an account by scanning the QR code, or type the setup key.
    2. Type the 6 digit code the app shows, and your password.
    3. Press Turn on two-factor sign in.

    From then on, signing in asks for a code after your password. Lost your phone? See Troubleshooting.

    Devices and activity

    Sign out everywhere else ends every other session at once, remembered ones too. A browser unused for {{ idle_days }} days is signed out by itself. Recent security activity lists your sign ins and account changes with their time and network address.

    Delete your account

    Delete my account removes the account with all its years, movements and receipts. It needs your password, two confirmations and your email typed. It cannot be undone, so download your accountant packages first.

    Troubleshooting

    No confirmation email, or the link expired

    Check your spam folder. Signing in with your email and password sends a new link. Emails go out at most once a minute per account. If {{ verify_minutes }} minutes have passed, sign up again with the same address.

    "Too many failed sign in attempts"

    Wait the minutes the message says, then try again, or use Forgot password?. The pause protects your account from password guessing.

    Lost the phone with your authenticator app

    Write to {{ support_email }} from your account's email address. Once we have checked it is you, we remove two-factor sign in so you can sign in with your password and turn it on again.

    A receipt is refused

    Only PDF, PNG, JPG, HEIC and WebP files are accepted, up to the per receipt size of your plan. Export other formats (Word, Pages, a web page) to PDF first. If the message says your storage is full, delete old receipts or upgrade.

    The day count looks wrong

    Look for "counted" under a movement in the table: it shares days with another one. Remember that empty future days count nowhere yet, and that both the first and the last day of a movement count. See How days are counted.
